The **Dev Console Pro** empowers you to execute functions, inspect variables, and manipulate your game's state at runtime—without modifying your scene hierarchy or importing heavy UI prefabs. 

Unlike standard UI toolkits, the console is generated automatically through pure IMGUI, meaning it works immediately upon dropping the package into your project. It includes a built-in Reflection Evaluator to parse raw C# expressions on the fly and features a smart, context-aware autocomplete engine to drastically speed up your debugging workflow.
